# Coinly Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-14_

Coinly ("we", "us") is a financial-literacy education app. Your privacy matters to
us. This policy explains what we collect, how we store it, and how it is used.

## Information we collect

When you create an account and use Coinly, we collect:

- **Email address** — used to create and sign in to your account.
- **Display name** — shown in your profile.
- **App usage and learning progress** — lessons completed, XP, streaks, Brain
  Bucks, mastery, review queue, and similar progress data.
- **Virtual portfolio data** — your simulated cash balance, holdings, simulated
  order history, and watchlist from the in-app paper-trading and game features.

We do **not** collect real financial account information. All trading, balances,
and "Brain Bucks" in Coinly are **simulated** and have no real-world monetary value.

## How your data is stored

Your data is stored in [Supabase](https://supabase.com) (our backend provider).
Each record is protected by **Row-Level Security (RLS)**, which restricts access
so that you can only read and write your own data — one user's information is
never accessible to another user. Data is also cached on your device so the app
works offline.

## How we use your data

- To create and authenticate your account.
- To save and sync your learning progress and virtual portfolio across your
  devices and reinstalls.
- To personalize the learning experience (for example, the in-app assistant uses
  a summary of your progress and simulated portfolio to give relevant guidance).

## What we do not do

- We **do not sell** your personal data to third parties.
- We **do not** share your personal data with advertisers.

## Third-party services

Coinly displays real-time market data from third-party sources, including
**Stooq** and **CoinGecko**. When fetching prices and charts, the app sends only
public ticker symbols (e.g. "AAPL", "bitcoin") to these services. **No personal
information and no part of your virtual portfolio is sent to these data
providers.**

Account data and progress are processed by **Supabase** as described above.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. To request deletion of your
account and associated data, contact us at the address below.

## Children's privacy

Coinly is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13.
